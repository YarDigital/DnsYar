# Privacy Policy — Dns Yar

_Last updated: 2026-11-24_

Dns Yar ("the app") is developed by YarDigital. This page explains what the app does and does not do with your data.

## No accounts, no ads, no tracking
Dns Yar has no user accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

## DNS requests
When you connect to a DNS server in the app, your phone's DNS requests are sent only to the DNS provider you chose. That provider's own privacy policy applies to those requests — Dns Yar does not see or log them.

## IP and domain lookup
The IP tab and the DNS tests use public lookup services (for example ipwho.is, ipify, Cloudflare, and bash.ws) to show your IP address, ISP, and DNS information. These requests go directly from your phone to those services.

## What stays on your device
Your DNS list, any servers you add or import, your settings, and your language/theme preference are stored only on your phone (local storage). They are never sent to us.

## Permissions
- **VPN permission**: used only to apply the DNS server you choose, locally on your phone. Dns Yar does not act as a general-purpose VPN and does not route your traffic through any server we control.
- **Notifications**: used only to show the status of the active DNS connection.
